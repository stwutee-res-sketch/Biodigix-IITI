Feedstock library

Indicative characteristics used as defaults. Methane potential varies with source, storage and pretreatment — measure it for a real project.

infoHow to read this

TS total solids (% of fresh mass); VS volatile solids (% of TS); BMP biochemical methane potential (Nm³ CH₄ per kg VS added), the ceiling a digester approaches but rarely reaches.

Ranges are typical literature values compiled from standard texts [17][18]; BMP assays should follow Angelidaki et al. (2009) [19], Holliger et al. (2016) [22] or VDI 4630 [21].

Lignocellulosic residues (paddy, wheat and soybean straw) usually need size reduction or pretreatment — a focus of STWUTEE group research at IIT Indore.

The IIT Indore field coefficient (0.34 m³ biogas/kg TS for cattle dung) comes from household-plant assessments in Madhya Pradesh, 2019–2024 [24].

Statement of novelty. An integrated system that dynamically links field-validated biological digestion parameters, reactor design outputs, techno-economic indicators and Indian government policy instruments within a single digital platform for the biogas and CBG domain, including a multi-stream revenue model (CBG + FOM + carbon) with simultaneous subsidy integration and DSCR/IRR/NPV assessment.
